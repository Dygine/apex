Demo 01
Demo project

Corporate campus

Challenge. A multi-block office campus running five separate service vendors, with no single point of accountability when something went wrong between them.

Approach. Consolidate all five into one integrated contract, keep the incumbent site staff who were performing well, and introduce a shared roster and one escalation path.

Services in scope

  • Housekeeping
  • Security
  • HVAC
  • Technical
  • Waste

Outcome

A single monthly report replaces five, and escalations have one owner. Illustrative outcome for presentation purposes.

Demo 02
Demo project

Residential community

Challenge. A gated development where resident complaints centred on inconsistent common-area upkeep and unclear visitor control at the gate.

Approach. Fixed daily schedules per block, a logged complaint route with closing times, and verified security staff briefed on the community's own access rules.

Services in scope

  • Housekeeping
  • Landscaping
  • Security
  • Pest control

Outcome

Complaints move from informal messages to a tracked register with visible closure. Illustrative outcome for presentation purposes.

Demo 03
Demo project

Industrial facility

Challenge. A production site needing permit-controlled electrical and hot work carried out without interrupting shift output or breaching safety procedure.

Approach. Shift-aligned deployment, permit and isolation discipline for every intervention, and waste handover routed through authorised recyclers with weight logs.

Services in scope

  • Electrical
  • Technical
  • Waste
  • Fire & safety

Outcome

Maintenance windows align to shift changeover rather than competing with it. Illustrative outcome for presentation purposes.

Demo 04
Demo project

Healthcare facility

Challenge. A hospital wing requiring infection-aware cleaning protocols, segregated waste streams and documentation that would stand up to inspection.

Approach. Zone-specific protocols with colour-coded equipment, 24/7 rostering, and a compliance record pack maintained continuously rather than assembled before audits.

Services in scope

  • Housekeeping
  • Waste
  • Pest control
  • Technical

Outcome

Documentation is current on any given day, not just audit week. Illustrative outcome for presentation purposes.
