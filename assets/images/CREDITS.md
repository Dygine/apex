## A note on the generated files

The Apex workforce images carry the small four-point sparkle mark that the
image generator adds to its output, usually in the bottom-right corner. It is
faint and it does not read as a logo at page scale, but it is there. Removing it
means either re-exporting without the mark or re-cropping past it.
