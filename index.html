    Every proposal is written after a site walk, so the numbers change. The structure does not.

    Deployed to a written roster, in Apex uniform, with photo ID and police verification on file.

    • Shift cover agreed before mobilisation, including reliever cover

    Read our approach

    A hospital corridor is not a warehouse floor.

    The two need different routines, different headcount and different timings. Scope is written around how your building is actually used, after we have walked it.

    How we take over a facility.

    A six-stage mobilisation that runs from the first site walk to a reviewed, improving operation. Handover runs in parallel, so nothing on your site stops while it happens.

    01

    Discover

    We walk the site, meet the people who use it, and understand what already works before proposing changes.

    02

    Assess

    Asset condition, manpower requirement, risk exposure and statutory gaps are recorded against each area of the building.

    03

    Plan

    A written scope, roster and maintenance calendar built for this facility, with costs and service levels agreed up front.

    04

    Deploy

    Trained teams mobilise with equipment, consumables and a named supervisor. Handover runs in parallel so nothing stops.

    05

    Monitor

    Checklists, spot audits and a response desk that logs every escalation with a closing time against it.

    06

    Improve

    A monthly review of what was completed, what slipped and what changes next month, agreed with your team.
