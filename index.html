    About Apex

    Behind every well-run facility.

    Bangalore · Karnataka Single contract

    Apex Integrated Facility Management runs the day-to-day operation of commercial, healthcare, industrial, institutional and residential premises. We take on the people, the schedules, the checklists and the reporting, so the building works without your team having to chase it.

    Most facilities do not fail dramatically. They drift — a skipped inspection here, an unfilled log there, three vendors each assuming the fourth handled it. Integrated management exists to close those gaps, because when every service sits inside one operating plan there is nowhere for work to fall between contracts.

    Our approach

    How we takeover a facility.

    A six-stage mobilisation that runs from the first site walk to a reviewed, improving operation.

    01

    Discover

    We walk the site, meet the people who use it, and understand what already works before proposing changes.

    02

    Assess

    Asset condition, manpower requirement, risk exposure and statutory gaps are recorded against each area of the building.

    03

    Plan

    A written scope, roster and maintenance calendar built for this facility, with costs and service levels agreed up front.

    04

    Deploy

    Trained teams mobilise with equipment, consumables and a named supervisor. Handover runs in parallel so nothing stops.

    05

    Monitor

    Checklists, spot audits and a response desk that logs every escalation with a closing time against it.

    06

    Improve

    A monthly review of what was completed, what slipped and what changes next month, agreed with your team.
