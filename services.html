Delivery process

The same sixstages, every time.

Whether you take one service or all six, mobilisation follows the same route from first site walk to monthly review.

01

Discover

Site walk, occupancy patterns, and what the current arrangement already does well.

02

Assess

Asset condition, manpower requirement, risk exposure and statutory gaps, recorded per area.

03

Plan

Written scope, roster and maintenance calendar with costs and service levels agreed up front.

04

Deploy

Trained teams mobilise with equipment and consumables under a named supervisor.

05

Monitor

Checklists, spot audits and a response desk that logs every escalation to closure.

06

Improve

Monthly review of what was completed, what slipped and what changes next month.
