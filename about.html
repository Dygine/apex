Our story

Company story

Facilities rarely fail loudly.

They drift. A filter that was not changed, a logbook nobody filled in, three vendors each assuming the fourth had handled it. By the time anyone notices, the problem is no longer a maintenance task — it is a complaint, an audit finding, or a shutdown.

Apex was set up around a straightforward idea: put every service under one operating plan, give the site a named supervisor, and write down what good looks like before the first shift starts. Nothing about that is complicated. It is simply harder to do consistently than most contracts allow for.

We work with building owners, facility managers and administration teams who would rather spend their attention on their own business than on coordinating vendors.

Mission, vision & values

What we holdourselves to.

Three commitments that shape how every Apex site is staffed, scheduled and reviewed.

Mission

Keep buildings working

To run facilities so reliably that the people using them stop having to think about it — and the people responsible for them stop having to chase it.

Vision

The standard in Karnataka

To be the integrated facility management partner that regional clients recommend because the service on an ordinary Tuesday matches the service on an audit day.

Values

Say it, then do it

Scope written plainly, work recorded honestly, problems raised early. We would rather report a miss than quietly absorb it.

Approach

Our approach

Every engagement starts with a site walk, not a proposal. We look at how the building is used, where the pressure points are and what the current team already does well, then design around that.

What we assess

  • Asset condition and age
  • Manpower deployed per shift
  • Statutory and compliance gaps
  • Complaint and breakdown history

What you receive

  • Written scope of work
  • Roster and maintenance calendar
  • Agreed service levels
  • Named account manager
People

Our people

Facility management is a people business before it is anything else. Most of what a client experiences comes down to whether the person on site that morning was trained, supervised and turned up.

How we staff

  • Local recruitment
  • Background verification
  • Role-specific induction
  • Refresher training on schedule

How we supervise

  • Named on-site supervisor
  • Shift handover records
  • Attendance visibility
  • Backup manpower pool
Safety

Safety

Risk assessment happens before the job rather than after the incident. Hazardous work runs under permit, protective equipment is standard issue, and records stay current whether or not anyone is auditing.

Controls

  • Pre-task risk assessment
  • Permit-controlled hot and electrical work
  • Lock-out and isolation checks
  • Incident reporting and review

Culture

  • Toolbox talks
  • PPE discipline
  • Near-miss reporting
  • Evacuation drills
Quality

Quality

Quality is defined before work starts and checked after. Standards are written per area, audited on a schedule, and corrective actions are tracked to closure rather than to acknowledgement.

How we measure

  • Area-wise checklists
  • Scheduled spot audits
  • Consumable tracking
  • Escalation closing times

How we report

  • Monthly service review
  • Open issue register
  • Compliance record pack
  • Agreed improvement actions
